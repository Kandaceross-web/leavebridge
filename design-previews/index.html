LeaveBridge Health · Homepage redesign

Three luxury design concepts

Each concept is a working, responsive homepage built from the same verified content: real prices from the service catalog, the real service area (Mississippi and Tennessee), the real clinical workflow and the existing refund rules. Anything not yet verified is marked with a yellow label. Photographs are licensed Pexels images used for illustration; they do not show LeaveBridge patients or clinicians.

Recommended

A · Editorial Private Care

Typography
Cormorant Garamond headlines at large editorial sizes with one italic accent word; Inter for body text at 17px.
Image treatment
One prominent window-lit portrait in a 4:5 frame with a fine inset rule. Everything else is type, hairlines and whitespace.
Layout
Asymmetric split hero, ruled trust strip, numbered three-column process, and an editorial price list that reads like a private practice fee schedule.
Customer journey
Headline, primary action and the $279.99 fee are all in the first screen; services and the clinician follow, then a sticky-heading FAQ.

Why Concept A

It reads most like an established private practice, which is the brief's core aim, while still answering what the service is, who it is for, the price and the next step in the first screen. Its system of hairlines, restrained radii and one strong photograph carries cleanly into intake forms, the patient dashboard and documents, where B's cards would also work but feel more like software, and C depends on a lot of photography the practice does not own yet. B's assessment summary panel and step cards are worth reusing inside the patient portal whichever concept you pick.

What was checked

  • All three pages at 1440px and 390px wide: no horizontal overflow.
  • Automated axe scan for WCAG 2.2 A and AA rules: no violations on any page at either width. This is an automated check, not a compliance claim.
  • Mobile menu opens and closes with the button and the Escape key; skip link, visible focus states and reduced-motion support are in place.

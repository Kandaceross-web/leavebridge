About the assessment

What is a mental health leave assessment?

It is a professional evaluation by a licensed mental-health clinician of how your symptoms are affecting your ability to work. The clinician decides independently whether leave documentation is clinically supported.

Who is this for?

Adults 18 and older whose mental-health symptoms may be affecting their work, who are located in a state where our clinicians are licensed, and who may need documentation for medical leave.

Does paying guarantee I will get documentation?

No. Your payment covers the professional assessment. The clinician may determine that documentation is not clinically supported, that more information is needed, or that a different kind of care is more appropriate.

FMLA and workplace leave

Does LeaveBridge decide whether I am eligible for FMLA?

No. FMLA eligibility depends on your employer and your employment history, and leave decisions are made by your employer or leave administrator. Our clinicians provide a clinical assessment and, when supported, medical documentation.

What is the difference between continuous and intermittent leave?

Continuous leave is a single block of time away from work. Intermittent leave is time off in separate periods as needed. A reduced schedule means working fewer hours. The clinician documents what is clinically supported, which may differ from what was requested.

Payment

How much does it cost?

The Mental Health Leave Assessment is $279.99, self-pay, and covers the assessment and its paperwork. A recertification visit is $119.99 and each follow-up form is $9.99. We do not bill insurance.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds depend on how far your case has progressed. The current cancellation and refund rules are shown on the pricing page and before you pay.

Documentation

What documentation can the clinician complete?

When clinically supported, the clinician can complete employer or leave-administrator medical certification forms and supporting letters within their professional scope.

Will my employer accept a form from your clinician?

Employers and leave administrators set their own rules about which provider types may complete their forms. Please check with your HR department or leave administrator before you pay.

What if my employer won't accept my form?

First, ask your employer exactly what is missing or why the form was not accepted, and send that request to us through your portal messages. If a form we completed is incomplete or unclear, your clinician will correct or clarify it at no extra charge. If your employer only accepts certain provider types, we can give you a copy of your assessment records to take to a provider your employer accepts. Refunds follow our refund policy, which is based on how far your case progressed, not on your employer's decision.

Telehealth

How do telehealth appointments work?

If your clinician needs to meet with you, you will schedule a secure video visit from your portal. You must be physically located in a state where the clinician is licensed during the visit.

Privacy

Will my employer see my health information?

No information is sent to your employer unless you authorize it. You choose which completed documents to share, and with whom.

How do you protect my information?

Health information is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to the people involved in your care, and every access is logged.

Appointments

When will I meet with a clinician?

After you submit your intake, a clinician reviews your information. If a video assessment is needed, you will be asked to choose an available time.

Employer forms

How do I share my employer's forms?

Upload them securely in your portal as PDF, JPG or PNG files. You will not be asked to upload the same document twice.

After the assessment

What happens after the assessment?

Your clinician records a determination and explains it in your portal. If documentation is clinically supported, it is completed, signed and delivered securely in your portal.

What if my employer denies my leave?

Leave decisions belong to your employer or leave administrator. LeaveBridge cannot appeal on your behalf. You may wish to talk with HR or an employment attorney.

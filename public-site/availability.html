Where we serve

Clinicians can only provide care to people who are physically located in a state where they are licensed.

States we currently serve

  • Mississippi
  • Tennessee

Your location during care matters

What counts is where you are physically located when you receive care, not where you live, work or are registered. You will confirm your location when you create an account and again before any video assessment.

If you will be travelling, complete your assessment while you are in a state we serve.

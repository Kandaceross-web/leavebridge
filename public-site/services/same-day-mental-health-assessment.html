Start the screening

What it is not

This is an automated screening record. It was generated by software from the person's own answers to standard screening questionnaires. No clinician examined the person, and the scores and summary were produced by software without clinician input. It is not a diagnosis, not a clinician-conducted examination, not an FMLA or other leave certification, and not a medical work excuse.

Paying does not mean you will receive any letter, diagnosis, certification or leave. Your employer or leave administrator decides about leave.

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Check we can help

    Two quick question sets about where you are and your safety.

  2. Step 2

    Create your account

    Then confirm your email address.

  3. Step 3

    Pay securely

    You see a written estimate first.

  4. Step 4

    Answer the questionnaires

    The PHQ-9, the GAD-7 or both, plus questions about symptoms, work and missed time.

  5. Step 5

    Get your record

    Your answers are scored automatically and your signed record is ready in your portal. We email you a link to sign in.

Need a work excuse letter?

After your record is issued you can ask for a work excuse letter. A licensed clinician reviews your screening and your request and decides whether to issue one. They may change it or decline. For FMLA forms or a longer absence, a clinician-led leave assessment is the right service.

If you are in crisis

This service is not for emergencies. If you are in danger, call 911 or call or text 988. See crisis resources.

Some answers, such as thoughts of self-harm, mean a clinician looks at your screening before the record is issued.

Anyone you share your record with can confirm it is genuine on our verification page, which never shows health information.

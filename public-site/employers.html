Employer services

A clear referral pathway for your people

When an employee asks where to get a mental-health leave assessment, you can point them to an independent clinical service with a dedicated link.

Who we work with

  • Employers and HR teams
  • Leave administrators
  • Correctional facilities
  • Schools and universities
  • Public agencies
  • Healthcare organizations
  • Employee assistance programs (EAPs)
  • Unions

What we offer

  • A referral pathway. A simple way to direct employees to an assessment by a licensed mental-health clinician.
  • A dedicated landing link. A page for your organization that explains the service in neutral terms and starts the same screening everyone uses.
  • Plain-language information your HR team can share about how the process works.

What stays with the patient

  • The patient alone decides what is shared, and with whom.
  • Employers never receive clinical information without the patient’s written authorization.
  • We do not tell your organization whether an individual employee used the link.
We do not make leave decisions, determine FMLA eligibility, or act as a leave administrator. Clinicians make independent clinical determinations only.

Ask about a referral link

Tell us about your organization and we will reply by email. Please do not include information about any individual employee.

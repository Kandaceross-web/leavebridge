Employer services

A clear referral pathway for your people

Point employees to an independent clinical assessment with one dedicated link.

Who we work with

  • Employers and HR teams
  • Leave administrators
  • Correctional facilities
  • Schools and universities
  • Public agencies
  • Healthcare organizations
  • Employee assistance programs (EAPs)
  • Unions

What we offer

A referral pathway

A simple way to send employees to a licensed mental-health clinician.

A dedicated landing link

A neutral page for your organization that starts the same screening everyone uses.

Plain-language info

Material your HR team can share about how the process works.

What stays with the patient

  • The patient alone decides what is shared, and with whom.
  • No clinical information reaches you without their written authorization.
  • We don’t tell you whether an employee used the link.

We don’t make leave decisions, determine FMLA eligibility, or act as a leave administrator. Clinicians make independent clinical determinations only.

Ask about a referral link

We reply by email. Please don’t include anything about an individual employee.

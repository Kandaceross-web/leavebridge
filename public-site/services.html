Types of leave documentation

When the clinician determines it is clinically supported, they can complete documentation for one of these arrangements, as appropriate to your situation. The clinician decides what, if anything, is supported.

Continuous leave

Time away from work for a single, unbroken period, such as several consecutive weeks.

Intermittent leave

Time off in separate blocks as symptoms or treatment require, such as occasional days or partial days.

Reduced schedule

Working fewer hours per day or fewer days per week for a period of time.

What can be completed

  • Your employer's or leave administrator's medical certification form, when you upload it
  • A clinician letter for continuous leave, intermittent leave or a reduced schedule
  • A letter explaining the clinician's determination

Documentation is completed only when the clinician finds it clinically supported. It is not completed automatically, and the outcome may be that no documentation is supported.

Check with HR first

Employers and leave administrators decide which provider types they accept for medical certification, and whether you are eligible for FMLA or other leave. Before you start, ask your HR team or leave administrator:

  • Whether they accept certification from a licensed mental-health clinician via telehealth
  • Which form they need and when it is due
  • Whether you are eligible for the leave you are requesting
We do not decide FMLA eligibility or approve leave. Those decisions belong to your employer or leave administrator.

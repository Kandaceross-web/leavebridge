Work & School Excuse Evaluation

$29.99

Request a confidential mental health evaluation for a work or school absence. Complete your intake online, provide information about your symptoms, and receive clear next steps regarding your documentation request.

View Service Details

Documentation requests are subject to appropriate clinical evaluation and approval. Screening completion alone does not guarantee an excuse.

Explore Your Leave Options

Mental health concerns affect everyone differently. Your evaluation helps determine what type of documentation, if any, may be clinically appropriate.

Continuous Leave

Evaluation of mental health concerns that may require an extended period away from work.

Intermittent Leave

Evaluation of conditions that may require periodic absences.

Reduced Work Schedule

Evaluation of mental health concerns that may affect the ability to maintain a regular work schedule.

Workplace Accommodations

Evaluation of clinically supported workplace adjustment requests.

Learn About Evaluation Requirements

What can be completed

  • Your employer's or leave administrator's certification form, when you upload it
  • A clinician letter for continuous, intermittent or reduced-schedule leave
  • A letter explaining the clinician's determination

Nothing is completed automatically. The outcome may be that no documentation is supported.

Check with HR first

Ask your HR team or leave administrator:

  • Do you accept certification from a licensed mental-health clinician by telehealth?
  • Which form do you need, and when is it due?
  • Am I eligible for the leave I'm requesting?

We don’t decide FMLA eligibility or approve leave. Your employer or leave administrator does.

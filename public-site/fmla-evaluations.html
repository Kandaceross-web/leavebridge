Support for Your Next Step.

Mental health conditions can affect attendance, concentration, productivity, and daily functioning.

Our evaluation process is designed to help determine whether your mental health concerns support a request for workplace leave or accommodations.

Every clinical determination is based on the information obtained during your evaluation.

Explore Your Leave Options

Mental health concerns affect everyone differently. Your evaluation helps determine what type of documentation, if any, may be clinically appropriate.

Continuous Leave

Evaluation of mental health concerns that may require an extended period away from work.

Intermittent Leave

Evaluation of conditions that may require periodic absences.

Reduced Work Schedule

Evaluation of mental health concerns that may affect the ability to maintain a regular work schedule.

Workplace Accommodations

Evaluation of clinically supported workplace adjustment requests.

What Your Evaluation Includes

Self-pay. No subscription, and we do not bill insurance.

  • Review of your intake, questionnaires and the forms you upload
  • A telehealth assessment with a licensed mental-health clinician when the clinician determines one is needed
  • The clinician's independent determination, explained in your portal
  • Completion of the paperwork for this leave request when the clinician finds it clinically supported
  • Secure delivery of completed documents through your portal

Clinical findings determine whether documentation can be issued. FMLA eligibility and employer approval are separate determinations. Pricing and refunds

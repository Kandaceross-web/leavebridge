Missed Work or School? Your Mental Health Matters, Too.

Not every difficult day comes with a fever.

Anxiety, stress, depression, and emotional exhaustion can affect your ability to work or attend school.

LeaveBridge Health provides a confidential online process for requesting an evaluation for mental health-related absence documentation.

$29.99

Request an Excuse Evaluation

Sometimes, You Need Time to Take Care of Yourself.

Mental health concerns can affect concentration, energy, emotional well-being, and everyday functioning.

If you’ve missed work or school because of your mental health, an appropriate professional evaluation can help determine whether supporting documentation is clinically warranted.

Our goal is to make the request process clear, confidential, and convenient.

How It Works

  1. 1

    Step 1: Tell Us About Your Absence

    Complete a confidential intake describing your symptoms, the date of your absence, and the documentation you’re requesting.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Complete Your Assessment

    Answer the required mental health screening questions and provide any additional information needed for evaluation.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Receive Your Outcome

    Receive your screening results and information about the documentation decision. When clinically supported and authorized, your documentation will be made available securely.

What You Receive

Each part of the service is different. Here is what each one is.

  • QuestionnaireYou complete standard screening questionnaires (PHQ-9, GAD-7 or both) and questions about your symptoms, work and missed time.
  • Screening resultsYour answers are scored by software and you receive a digitally signed Automated Mental Health Screening Record. It is not a clinician examination, a diagnosis or an excuse.
  • Clinician reviewYou may then request a work or school excuse letter. A licensed clinician reviews your screening and your request individually, without a video visit, and may approve, change or decline it.
  • DocumentationIf the clinician approves, a signed letter with a verifiable document number is made available in your secure portal. Anyone you share it with can check it is genuine and unchanged.

If You Are in Crisis

This service is not for emergencies. If an answer about safety holds your screening, a clinician reviews it on the next business day, not in real time. If you are in danger, call 911 or call or text 988. See crisis resources.

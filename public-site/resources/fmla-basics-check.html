FMLA basics check

Three questions about your job, not your health. Your answers stay in your browser: nothing is sent, saved or tracked.

Pending review. This check describes the federal FMLA eligibility rules and is not final until signed off.
Does your employer have 50 or more employees working within 75 miles of your worksite?

Public agencies and schools are covered by FMLA at any size, but this 50-employee rule still applies to whether you are eligible.

Have you worked for this employer for at least 12 months in total?

The months don't need to be in a row.

Have you worked at least 1,250 hours in the last 12 months?

That's about 24 hours a week. Paid time off doesn't count toward it.

General information, not legal advice. Some jobs (for example airline flight crews) have special rules, and state laws may give more leave. The Department of Labor explains the full rules.

Need medical documentation for leave?

A licensed clinician can assess how your symptoms affect your work. See who can complete FMLA forms first.

Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to learn about our services, complete an assessment and read their documents, including people who use assistive technology.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA across this website and the patient portal.

How we check

  • Every change is tested with automated accessibility checks on phone-sized and desktop screens.
  • Pages are built to work with a keyboard alone, with screen readers, and when text is enlarged.
  • We keep color contrast high and do not rely on color alone to show meaning.

Automated checks cannot catch every barrier, and the site has not yet been reviewed by an independent accessibility auditor. If something does not work for you, please tell us.

Tell us about a barrier

Email support@leavebridgehealth.com (Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central). Let us know the page, what you were trying to do and the device or assistive technology you use. Please do not include health details in your email. We will work with you to provide the information or complete the step another way.

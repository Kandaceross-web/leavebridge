About LeaveBridge Health

A telehealth service for adults whose mental-health symptoms may be affecting their ability to work, built around an independent clinical assessment.

Independent judgment

Every determination is made by a licensed clinician using their own professional judgment. No software makes clinical decisions, and the fee is the same whatever the outcome.

Licensed where you are

Clinicians are licensed in the states they serve, and you are matched with a clinician licensed where you are physically located during care.

Your records, your choice

Your information stays in a private portal. You decide who receives any documents, and nothing is shared with an employer without your written authorization.

What we do, and what we don’t

We provide assessments and, when clinically supported, leave documentation. We do not provide emergency care, ongoing therapy or medication management, and we do not decide FMLA eligibility or approve leave; those decisions belong to your employer or leave administrator.

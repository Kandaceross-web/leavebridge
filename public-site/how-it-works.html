How it works

Seven steps, one secure portal

From a two-minute screening to secure delivery, here is what happens and who does what.

  1. Answer a few screening questionsA short set of questions confirms you are 18 or older, located in a state we serve, and looking for the kind of help we provide. No account or payment is needed to check.
  2. Create your account, verify your identity and confirm your locationYou upload a photo ID and confirm where you are physically located. Clinicians can only provide care in states where they are licensed, so your location matters.
  3. Pay $279.99 securelyPayment is collected after you choose your service and before your secure intake opens. Payment covers the professional assessment, not a particular result. Pricing and refunds
  4. Complete your secure intakeYou describe your history and how symptoms are affecting work, complete two short standardized questionnaires, and upload any employer or leave-administrator forms. You can save and come back.
  5. A licensed clinician reviews your case (clinician-only step)A clinician licensed in your state reads everything you submitted. They may ask you for more information or schedule a secure video assessment.
  6. The clinician makes an independent determination (clinician-only step)Using their own professional judgment, the clinician decides whether leave documentation is clinically supported, and explains the outcome in your portal. The outcome may be that documentation is not supported.
  7. Signed documents are delivered securelyIf the clinician completes documentation, the signed documents appear in your portal for you to download. You decide who receives them; we do not send anything to your employer on our own.
Clinical steps are clinician-only. Steps 5 and 6 (shaded) are carried out by a licensed clinician alone. No software makes clinical decisions, and payment never determines the outcome.

How long it takes

Most people can start their intake the same day they create an account. Clinical timelines depend on your case.

If you have a documentation deadline, tell us during screening so the clinical team can see it.

You control sharing

Your records stay in your private portal. Nothing is released to an employer, insurer or leave administrator without your written authorization.

Emergencies are never handled through this process

LeaveBridge Health is not an emergency or crisis service and cannot respond in real time. Please contact one of these resources right away.

  • Call or text 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, available 24/7 in the U.S.
  • Call 911 — For immediate danger or a medical emergency.
  • Go to the nearest emergency room — If you can do so safely.
  • Mississippi: 1-877-210-8513 — Mississippi Department of Mental Health helpline.
  • Tennessee: 855-274-7471 — Tennessee Statewide Crisis Line, available 24/7.

How it works

Seven steps, one secure portal

From a two-minute check to documents in your hands.

  1. 1

    Answer a few questions

    About two minutes. No account or payment.

    More detail

    We confirm you are 18 or older, located in a state we serve, and looking for the kind of help we provide.

  2. 2

    Verify your ID and location

    Upload a photo ID and confirm where you are.

    More detail

    Clinicians can only provide care in states where they are licensed, so where you are physically located matters.

  3. 3

    Pay $279.99 securely

    One flat fee for the assessment, not a result.

    More detail

    Payment is collected after you choose your service and before your secure intake opens. Pricing and refunds

  4. 4

    Complete your intake

    Your history, two short questionnaires, your forms.

    More detail

    Describe how symptoms affect work and upload any employer or leave-administrator forms. You can save and come back.

  5. 5

    A clinician reviews your case (clinician-only step)

    They may ask questions or book a video visit.

    Clinician only
    More detail

    A clinician licensed in your state reads everything you submitted.

  6. 6

    An independent decision (clinician-only step)

    The clinician decides, and explains why.

    Clinician only
    More detail

    Using their own professional judgment, the clinician decides whether leave documentation is clinically supported. The outcome may be that it is not.

  7. 7

    Secure delivery

    Signed documents in your portal. You share them.

    More detail

    You decide who receives your documents. We do not send anything to your employer on our own.

Clinical steps are clinician-only

No software makes clinical decisions. Payment never decides the outcome.

How long it takes

Most people can start their intake the same day they create an account. Clinical timelines depend on your case. Have a deadline? Tell us at screening so the clinical team can see it.

You control sharing

Nothing goes to an employer, insurer or leave administrator without your written authorization.

Not for emergencies

LeaveBridge Health is not an emergency or crisis service and cannot respond in real time. Please contact one of these resources right away.

  • Call or text 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, available 24/7 in the U.S.
  • Call 911 — For immediate danger or a medical emergency.
  • Go to the nearest emergency room — If you can do so safely.
  • Mississippi: 1-877-210-8513 — Mississippi Department of Mental Health helpline.
  • Tennessee: 855-274-7471 — Tennessee Statewide Crisis Line, available 24/7.

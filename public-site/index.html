You need paperwork, and the usual routes aren’t working

“HR gave me a form and a deadline.”

Tell us your deadline during screening so the clinical team can see it. We can’t promise a date, and we’ll be honest if it’s tight.

“I don’t have a regular doctor.”

You don’t need a referral or an existing provider. A licensed clinician assesses you from the start.

“My therapist says they can’t fill it out.”

Many therapy services don’t complete employer forms. Completing them after a proper assessment is all we do.

Who this is for

Adults 18 and older whose anxiety, depression, burnout, or other mental-health symptoms are making work difficult, and who may need medical documentation for continuous leave, intermittent leave, or a reduced schedule.

You must be physically located in a state where our clinicians are licensed when you receive care. We currently serve Mississippi, Tennessee. Where we serve

How it works

Seven steps, one secure portal

  1. Answer a few questionsTwo minutes to confirm we can help and that you are in a state we serve.
  2. Create your account and verify your identityUpload a photo ID and confirm where you are located.
  3. Pay $279.99 securelyPayment is collected after you choose your service and before your secure intake opens.
  4. Complete your secure intakeYour history, how symptoms affect work, two short questionnaires, and any employer forms.
  5. A licensed clinician reviews your caseThey may ask for more information or schedule a video assessment.
  6. Independent clinical determinationThe clinician decides whether documentation is clinically supported, and explains the outcome.
  7. Secure deliverySigned documents appear in your portal. You decide who receives them.
Your clinician’s judgment is independent. Steps 5 and 6 are made by a licensed clinician alone. No software makes clinical decisions, and payment never determines the outcome.

Two things to know before you start

Pending legal review. The statements in this section describe how employers and FMLA rules may treat documentation and are not final until reviewed.

What your employer sees

You decide who receives your documents. Employer forms generally ask about the condition’s effect on your work and the time needed, and you can read everything in your portal before you share it.

We never contact your employer unless you ask us to, and we don’t bill insurance.

Who completes your forms

Forms are completed by a licensed mental-health clinician. FMLA rules list which provider types may certify leave, and some employers and benefit plans accept only certain ones.

Check with your HR team or leave administrator that they accept your clinician’s license type before you pay. Read more in our FAQ

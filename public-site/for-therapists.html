For therapists and EAPs

When a client needs leave paperwork you don’t complete

Refer them for an independent assessment, and keep therapy separate from the paperwork.

How referral works

  1. 1

    Share the link

    Our site or your practice’s own link. Your client screens and books.

  2. 2

    Independent assessment

    A licensed clinician decides if documentation is clinically supported.

  3. 3

    Only with consent

    We may ask you for treatment history only with your client’s written authorization. Never without it.

What we don’t do

  • Provide ongoing therapy or take over your client’s care
  • Promise any outcome to your client

Ask for a referral link

We reply by email. Please don’t include anything about a specific client.

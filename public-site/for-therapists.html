For therapists and EAPs

When a client needs leave paperwork you don't complete

Many therapy practices and employee assistance programs don't complete employer leave forms. You can refer clients here for an independent assessment, and keep your therapeutic relationship separate from the paperwork.

How referral works

  • Share our site or your practice’s own referral link. Your client screens and books on their own.
  • A licensed clinician assesses them independently and decides whether documentation is clinically supported.
  • With your client’s written authorization, the clinician may ask you for treatment history. We never contact you without it.

What we don’t do

  • Provide ongoing therapy or take over your client’s care
  • Promise any outcome to your client

Ask for a referral link

Tell us about your practice and we will reply by email. Please don’t include anything about a specific client.
